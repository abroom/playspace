How to play

  • Click a tower to pick up its revealed animals, or reveal its topmost hidden animal.
  • Click another tower to drop onto: it must be empty, have a hidden top animal, or a top animal that matches. You can also drag a tower straight to its destination instead of clicking twice.
  • Use crates to temporarily hold an animal.
  • Sort all nine towers.
  • Keyboard: number keys jump to a tower (0 for tower 10), arrows or WASD move between towers and crates, Space picks up or drops, and Backspace undoes. Hold Shift to jump 3 towers at a time, or straight to the next tower you can drop onto if you're holding a crate.

Cost

  • Each move costs $1.
  • An undo costs $10.
  • Another crate costs $100.
