FreeCell

Every card dealt face-up — four free cells and a clear head are all you get.

How to play

  • All 52 cards deal face-up across 8 tableau columns — nothing is hidden.
  • 4 free cells each hold one card. 4 foundations build up by suit, Ace to King.
  • Tableau columns build down in alternating colors. Unlike Klondike, any card can go on an empty column, not just a King.
  • Click, or press and drag, a card to pick it up (or the whole run above it, if it's a valid alternating sequence), then click/drop a destination pile. Click the same card again to put it back down.
  • Double-click a card to send it wherever it best fits: its foundation, then another legal tableau spot, then an empty free cell.
  • Arrow keys move a cursor between the free cells/foundations row and the tableau; Space does whatever a click at the cursor would.
  • Moving several cards at once (a "supermove") is limited by how many free cells and empty columns you have: you can move (1 + empty free cells) × 2empty columns cards at a time (an empty destination column doesn't count toward its own capacity).
  • Undo any move, any number of times — each one is tallied. Give up any time to see how you did; either way, the final screen shows cards remaining and undos used.
  • Keyboard: U undoes. Escape puts down a held card, or starts a new game if nothing's held (or closes whatever's open). Backspace or B goes back to the Playspace index.
