How to play

  • Each hand deals all 52 cards, 13 to each player. Before play, you pass 3 cards: left, then right, then across, then no pass — repeating in that order every 4 hands.
  • Whoever holds the 2 of clubs leads the first trick. Follow the led suit if you can; if you can't, play anything.
  • The highest card of the led suit wins the trick and leads the next one. There's no trump suit.
  • Hearts can't be led until one has already been played (voided off onto another trick) — unless hearts are all you have left.
  • No hearts or the Queen of Spades can be played on the very first trick, unless you have nothing else.
  • Each heart you take is 1 point; the Queen of Spades is 13 points. Lower is better.
  • Shooting the moon: take every heart and the Queen yourself, and you score 0 while everyone else takes 26.
  • Play continues until someone reaches 100. The lowest score wins.
  • Keyboard: ←/→ move over your hand, Space selects a card to pass or plays it. While passing, ↓ drops to the Pass button and ↑ returns. While playing, ↑/↓ jump straight to your first/last legal card.
  • Escape opens the new-game menu. Backspace or B goes back to the Playspace index.
