How to play

  • Click a hidden square to reveal it. A number tells you how many mines touch it.
  • Reveal a mine and it's game over: the one you hit turns red, mines you'd flagged correctly turn green, and flags you got wrong turn yellow.
  • Right-click a hidden square to cycle it: blank → 🚩 flagged → ❓ unsure → blank.
  • Clear every square that isn't a mine to win.
  • The 💡 button (or H) reveals a random safe square for you (3 on Medium, 5 on Big), next to what you've already uncovered where it can. It's disabled once the game is over or paused. Hints used are tallied in the yellow counter, continues in the blue one.
  • Hit a mine and only that square reveals — the rest stay hidden. You can then Reveal (or V) to see every mine and flag, or Continue (or C) to turn the mine you hit into a flag and keep playing.
  • The ⏱️ button (or P) pauses the timer and blurs the field so you can step away without giving anything up.
  • Keyboard: arrows or WASD move the cursor, Space reveals, F cycles flag/unsure, R restarts the same size.
  • Backspace or B goes back to the Playspace index.
  • The smiley restarts the same size too. The New game button lets you pick a different one. Escape opens the size picker (and pauses, if you weren't already) if nothing's open, or closes whatever's open (resuming, if Escape's the one that paused it). In that dialog, 1/2/3 pick Small/Medium/Big.
  • The first square you reveal is always safe — mines aren't placed until then.
